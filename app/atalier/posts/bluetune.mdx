---

I built this with some friends at HackTech back in 2014. BlueTune lets you pair a bunch of iPhones together 
and then play the same song in sync through all the phones. I thought it was a pretty neat way to share new music with friends. 
We also just wanted to play around with the new CoreBluetooth API that Apple had released.

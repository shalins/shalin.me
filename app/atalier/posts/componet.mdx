CompoNet is a research project I worked on with [Dr. Nathan Brooks](https://www.linkedin.com/in/nathan-brooks-29a887aa/) 
to visualize passive components like capacitors and inductors at a large, comprehensive scale in order to compare the
technologies across various figures of merit.

[](https://compo-net.org "preview")

Funnily, I actually pursued this as an unpaid project at U.C. Berkeley _after_ I had already graduated, 
mostly because I had taken a photovoltaics class my senior year which really left an imprint on me, 
and I wanted to stay involved in sustainable energy. The tool I would build with CompoNet would help
engineers design more efficient power electronics, for example higher energy density electric aircraft 
batteries where weight consideration is critical.

Through this, I ended up learning a lot about the world of academia, and was fortunate to get to present 
my work at PECI 2024.

Spacebar is a 3d world I built with my friends [Sam Gorman](https://samgorman.me/) and 
[Kanyes Thaker](https://kany.es/) over the course of 2+ years, which helps people build
together online in a natural way.

[](https://spacebar.build "preview")

While working on Spacebar, we used to have weekly syncs where we discussed which direction to go in
next, and since we lived in different cities, we'd use Spacebar to host those meetings. I always thought 
it was super cool that we used Spacebar to build Spacebar.

We designed and built the 3D spacebar model completely from scratch in Blender, then optimized the crap
out of it to get it to perform real-time in browser on older hardware. We wrote a custom WebRTC networking
stack, then shifted over to [LiveKit](https://livekit.io/) after running into issues. AI got so good 
throughout the process of building this that we went from a god awful skybox generated with 
[DALL-E](https://openai.com/index/dall-e/) to [Blockade Labs](https://www.blockadelabs.com/).

We never intended to work on this with such a strong consumer focus -- it was actually started purely
as a means to challenge ourselves as engineers and learn across new domains. But I guess we couldn't help
but care deeply about the product design and messaging, even though we ultimately decide to
stop working on it right as we geared up for a launch, to create time to pursue goals we thought were 
ultimately more important.
